# FunctionalOverkill Brand Notes

## Name

**FunctionalOverkill**  
Creator mark: **Fo** — pronounced *foe*.

Primary line:

> **Solving unnecessarily specific problems properly.**

Workshop rule:

> **It's not stupid if it fucking works.**

Project sign-off:

> **Here. Fixed your problem. Have a laugh too.**

The primary line is the public/professional tagline. The workshop rule is intentionally informal and should not replace the primary line on formal documentation, product labels, or manufacturing files.

## Canonical mark

The canonical mark is the striped **Fo** monogram: uppercase **F**, lowercase **o**.

The horizontal stripe construction is intrinsic to the logo.

**Do not:**

- remove or rearrange the stripes;
- stretch or skew the mark;
- add outlines, bevels, drop shadows, or decorative effects;
- redraw the geometry because a different version is more convenient;
- turn the lowercase `o` into an uppercase `O`.

The canonical full-color source is [`assets/Fo_Master_FullColor.svg`](assets/Fo_Master_FullColor.svg).

## Typography

Preferred type family: **IBM Plex**.

- **IBM Plex Mono Bold** — titles, equipment IDs, labels, revision marks
- **IBM Plex Sans** — body copy and headings
- **IBM Plex Mono** — commands, paths, configuration, technical callouts

Fonts are referenced by name; font files are not redistributed from this repository unless their license and packaging are handled separately.

## Manufacturing use

PCB silkscreen, engraving, embossing, laser marking, and other single-color production processes should use a monochrome version of the mark.

Manufacturing art should prioritize reliable reproduction over decorative effects.

## Voice

FunctionalOverkill should sound like the same person who designed the hardware:

- technically competent without pretending complexity is a personality;
- direct and specific;
- willing to explain the reason behind a decision;
- skeptical of features that exist only for marketing;
- comfortable with humor;
- professional where the context needs it;
- never afraid to call unnecessary bullshit unnecessary bullshit.

The joke should not obscure the engineering, and the engineering should not sterilize the joke.

## Open-source identity

When a Fo project is called open, the intention is practical reproducibility: editable source files, not merely exports chosen to prevent modification.

Project-specific licenses and third-party restrictions always override the general brand intent.
